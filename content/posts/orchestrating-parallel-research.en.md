## Overview

Concurrent experiments can conflict when agents share files, environments, or output directories. This article introduces Orchestrating Parallel Research and its workflow for task decomposition, workspace isolation, dispatch, and observation.

It discusses independent branches and artifact directories, using task reports to guide subsequent experiments, and coordinating parallel research on remote compute resources.
