## Overview

This article introduces the sketch-tech-illustration Skill and its approach to consistent AI-assisted visual production. It translates a warm, hand-drawn style into explicit requirements for color, linework, shapes, and composition.

It also covers reviewing generated work and adapting the palette while retaining a coherent style across illustrations, presentations, and storyboards.
