## Overview

Frontend validation depends on business logic, interaction states, and visual layout. This article introduces the Playwright CLI Skill and its use of navigation, clicks, form input, and screenshots to inspect actual browser behavior.

It also describes capturing key steps and annotating interface actions for user guides and visual debugging.
